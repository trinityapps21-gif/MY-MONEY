Frequently asked questions

How do I join an existing group?

Ask a group official for the invitation link. Open it, fill in your details and apply. Once an official approves you, you're in.

I forgot my password. What now?

If you saved your recovery code, use it on the Recover account page to set a new password straight away. Otherwise request a reset: a group official confirms it's you and sends a link that works for 24 hours, once.

I lost my recovery code.

Sign in, open your Profile and generate a new one (you'll need your current password). A new code is also issued at each sign-in.

Can other groups see my group's information?

No. Every group's members, savings and loans are completely isolated from every other group.

I made a mistake in a record. Can it be fixed?

Yes. Records are never silently changed. An official corrects it with a reversal entry, so the history stays complete and trustworthy.

Can I export reports?

Yes. Reports can be downloaded as PDF, Excel or CSV.
