How it works

Getting your group running takes a few minutes.

1

Register your group

Create your group, set its currency and add your logo. You become the group administrator.

2

Invite members

Share an invitation link. New members apply, and an official approves them before they join.

3

Record and lend

Record savings, approve loans and log repayments. Receipts and the ledger update automatically.

4

Report and share

Open live dashboards, export reports and keep everyone informed with announcements.

What every user should know

A few things that keep your account and your group's money safe.

  • Save your recovery codeOn first sign-in you get a one-time recovery code. Keep it safe. It lets you reset your password without waiting for anyone.
  • Forgot your password?Send a request and a group official will confirm it is really you, then send a reset link valid for 24 hours.
  • Corrections are visibleThe ledger is never rewritten. A mistake is fixed with a reversal entry, so the full history stays honest.
  • Each loan keeps its own termsChanging platform interest defaults only affects new loans, never loans already issued.
  • Your data stays in your groupNo other group can see your members, savings or loans.
